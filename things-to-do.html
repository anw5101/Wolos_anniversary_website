Ten Years Later, Let’s Celebrate Together!

We said “I do” on July 1, 2017, along the Mississippi River in Dubuque. A decade later—filled with unforgettable travels across Europe and beyond, a move to the Des Moines metro, and the adventure with our sweet son, Colin—we are so excited to gather our family and friends for a relaxed summer anniversary weekend!

If you’re in town on Friday evening, July 9, starting at 5:00 PM, come over to our house in Urbandale where the kids can play and we’ll have food and drinks. Then on Saturday, July 10, from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, join us at Big Grove Brewery & Taproom in downtown Des Moines for an easygoing afternoon of live music, food, and drinks!
